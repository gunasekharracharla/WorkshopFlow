FAQ

Know what you’re getting.

A clear view of today’s product scope and the current delivery model.

Is WorkshopFlow a self-service SaaS today?

No. The current product is a single-organization deployment configured for one workshop at a time. Multi-tenant accounts, subscription billing, and self-service onboarding are future product work.

Which payment provider and currency are supported?

The current checkout uses Razorpay and is configured for INR. Payment credentials and signature verification are handled by the backend.

Does WorkshopFlow send email reminders by itself?

No. Payment events and reminder requests can be connected to an external automation service. The organizer must configure that service to schedule and deliver messages.

Can I see the attendee experience?

Yes. Open the attendee page to see the configured workshop landing page and registration flow. It may connect to a live payment account, so only complete checkout if you intend to register for that workshop.
